# One-Click Deploy 🚀

Deploy ReachOut to production with a single click!

---

## Deploy to Vercel (Recommended)

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/jveldheer/ReachOut&env=ANTHROPIC_API_KEY&envDescription=Your%20Anthropic%20API%20key%20for%20email%20generation&project-name=reachout&repository-name=reachout)

**After deploying:**
1. Add your Anthropic API key in environment variables
2. Visit your deployed URL
3. Click "Brand Settings" and fill in your data
4. Start generating pitches!

---

## Deploy to Railway

[![Deploy on Railway](https://railway.app/button.svg)](https://railway.app/new/template?template=https://github.com/jveldheer/ReachOut)

**After deploying:**
1. Add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in environment variables
2. Generate a domain in Railway dashboard
3. Visit your app and configure brand settings

---

## Deploy to Render

[![Deploy to Render](https://render.com/images/deploy-to-render-button.svg)](https://render.com/deploy?repo=https://github.com/jveldheer/ReachOut)

**After deploying:**
1. Add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in environment section
2. Wait for deploy to complete (2-3 minutes)
3. Visit your `.onrender.com` URL

## What You'll Need

1. **GitHub Account** - To clone/fork the repo
2. **Hosting Account** - Free tier on Vercel, Railway, or Render
3. **Anthropic API Key** - Get from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys)

---

## After Deployment Checklist

- [ ] Add `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` environment variable
- [ ] Visit deployed URL and verify app loads
- [ ] Click "Brand Settings" and fill in your data
- [ ] Save brand settings
- [ ] Generate a test pitch to verify everything works
- [ ] Share URL with your team
- [ ] Start generating pitches!
